01What gets checked for every address每个地址都查什么

Checked查的

  • Syntax — is it shaped like an email address at all
  • Domain existence and MX health — does the domain exist, and does it route mail
  • Disposable domains — throwaway providers flagged per address
  • Role accounts — admin@, support@, postmaster@ and similar team addresses
  • Likely typos, with a correction — gmial.com → gmail.com, returned as a suggestion you can apply
  • Catch-all risk — a per-address signal (possible / unknown), labelled as a signal, not a probed fact
  • Domain age — in days, from RDAP registration records; null when no record exists, never estimated
  • Free-provider flag and a 0–100 score — the score starts at 100 and is reduced for each risk signal above
  • 格式——是不是一个邮箱地址的样子
  • 域名存在与 MX 健康——域名在不在、收不收信
  • 一次性域名——临时邮箱服务商逐条标记
  • 角色账号——admin@、support@、postmaster@ 这类团队地址
  • 疑似拼写错误并给纠正——gmial.com → gmail.com，以 suggestion 返回、可直接采用
  • Catch-all 风险——逐条信号（possible / unknown），明说是信号不是探测结论
  • 域名年龄——天数，来自 RDAP 注册记录；无记录返 null，从不估算
  • 免费服务商标记与 0–100 分——从 100 起、按上面每个风险信号扣分

Not checked — the honest boundary不查的——诚实边界

  • Whether a specific mailbox exists. No SMTP probing: major providers reject or greylist it, so a "guaranteed mailbox" claim from any checker is overselling.
  • Whether a catch-all address belongs to a real person. Only sending mail could tell; you get the risk signal instead.
  • Anything the response doesn't list. Every response carries scope: "domain-level" and a note stating exactly what was checked.
  • 某个具体邮箱是否存在。不做 SMTP 探测：主流服务商会拒绝或灰名单它，任何校验器「保证邮箱存在」的说法都是过度承诺。
  • Catch-all 地址是不是真人。只有真发信能确认；这里给风险信号代替。
  • 响应里没列的，一概没查。每个响应都带 scope: "domain-level" 和一段说明，把查过的范围写死。

07FAQ常见问题

Does this bulk email checker verify that a mailbox exists?这个批量校验会验证邮箱本体存在吗？

No. EmailCheck works at domain level: syntax, domain and MX health, disposable and role-account signals, likely typos and catch-all risk. It never probes a mailbox over SMTP — major providers reject or greylist that, so tools that promise mailbox certainty are overselling. A valid verdict means the domain is set up to receive mail and no risk signal fired, not that a specific mailbox is real.

不会。EmailCheck 在域名层工作：格式、域名与 MX 健康、一次性与角色账号信号、疑似拼写错误、catch-all 风险。它从不通过 SMTP 探测邮箱本体——主流服务商会拒绝或灰名单这种探测，承诺「邮箱确定存在」的工具都在过度销售。valid 裁决表示域名具备收信条件且没有风险信号触发，不表示某个具体邮箱真实存在。

How many emails can I check at once?一次能验多少个？

100 per run on this page, and 100 per API call. Paste or upload a longer list and only the first 100 are checked, with a note saying so — split longer lists into chunks of 100. A bad entry never fails the batch; it comes back as that row's invalid verdict.

本页每轮 100 条，API 每次调用也是 100 条。粘贴或上传更长的名单时只验前 100 条，并会明示——更长的名单请按每 100 条分片。坏条目不连坐整批，它就是自己那一行的 invalid 裁决。

Can a catch-all email address be verified?Catch-all 地址能被验证吗？

Not to mailbox certainty — by anyone. On a catch-all domain the server accepts every address at the door, so only sending real mail could tell. What you get here is a catch-all risk signal per address (possible or unknown), so you can segment those addresses instead of treating them as confirmed. See: can a catch-all email address be verified?

没法验到邮箱本体层面——谁都做不到。Catch-all 域名的服务器在门口对所有地址都收，只有真发信才能确认。这里给的是逐条 catch-all 风险信号（possible 或 unknown），让你把这些地址分段处理，而不是当作已确认。详见：catch-all 邮箱地址到底能不能验证。

Why do checkers charge for unknown or catch-all results?为什么校验器对 unknown 和 catch-all 结果也收费？

Because the checks behind them — DNS, MX, registration records — were still run and cost the same. We follow the same rule and say so up front: an unknown catch-all risk means the signals ran out, not that the work was skipped. See: why do verifiers charge full price for unknown results?

因为它们背后的检查——DNS、MX、注册记录——照样跑了、成本一样。我们也是这个规矩并且提前说清：catch-all 风险为 unknown 表示信号到头了，不是活儿没干。详见：为什么校验器对 unknown 结果也收全价。

What happens to the list I paste or upload?我粘贴或上传的名单会怎样？

The file is read in your browser; only the addresses are sent, over HTTPS, to the EmailCheck API, and the results come back to this page. The page keeps no copy — closing the tab clears the list — and submitted addresses are not added to any mailing list.

文件在你的浏览器里读取；只有地址本身经 HTTPS 发到 EmailCheck API，结果回到本页。本页不留副本——关掉标签页名单就清了——提交的地址也不会被加进任何邮寄名单。

What does a risky verdict mean — should I still send?Risky 裁决是什么意思——还能发吗？

Risky (score 50–79) means the domain routes mail but something is off: a disposable domain, a catch-all risk, or similar signals. Fix what is fixable, segment the rest, and keep risky addresses out of a first send to a cold list. See: what does a risky email verdict mean?

Risky（50–79 分）表示域名收信、但有问题：一次性域名、catch-all 风险或类似信号。能修的先修，其余分段，冷名单首发送别带 risky 地址。详见：risky 裁决是什么意思。
