01What it checks它查什么

Checked, per address每个地址都查的

  • Syntax and domain existence
  • MX records — does the domain route mail at all
  • Disposable-domain and role-account signals
  • Likely typos, with a correction (gmial.com → gmail.com)
  • Domain age in days, from RDAP registration records (null when no record exists — never estimated)
  • Catch-all risk signal, per address (possible / unknown)
  • A 0–100 score and a verdict: valid (≥80 with MX), risky (50–79, or a likely typo), invalid
  • 格式与域名是否存在
  • MX 记录——域名到底收不收信
  • 一次性域名与角色账号信号
  • 疑似拼写错误并给纠正建议（如 gmial.com → gmail.com）
  • 域名年龄（天），来自 RDAP 注册记录（无记录返 null，从不估算）
  • Catch-all 风险信号，逐条给出（possible / unknown）
  • 0–100 分与裁决：valid（≥80 且有 MX）、risky（50–79，或疑似拼错）、invalid

02How it works怎么用

  1. Send one address to GET /validate?email=... (or POST /validate with a JSON body).
  2. Read the verdict, the score, and the individual checks behind it — nothing is a bare true/false.
  3. For a list, POST /validate/batch with up to 100 addresses returns per-address results plus summary counts and percentages. A bad entry marks only itself; it never fails the batch.
  1. 把单个地址发给 GET /validate?email=...（或用 JSON body 走 POST /validate）。
  2. 读裁决、分数和它背后的逐项检查——不是只有一个 true/false。
  3. 整批名单用 POST /validate/batch，一次最多 100 条，返回逐条结果加 summary 计数与百分比。坏条目只标自己，不连坐整批。

05FAQ常见问题

What does EmailCheck check?EmailCheck 到底查什么？

Format and syntax, whether the domain exists and accepts mail (MX), disposable and role-account signals, likely typos with a suggested correction, domain age from registration records, and catch-all risk — combined into a 0–100 score and a valid / risky / invalid verdict.

格式与语法、域名是否存在且能收信（MX）、一次性与角色账号信号、疑似拼写错误及纠正建议、来自注册记录的域名年龄、catch-all 风险——合成 0–100 分与 valid / risky / invalid 裁决。

What does the score mean?分数是什么意思？

A 0–100 domain risk score. It starts at 100 and is reduced for missing MX, unknown domains, disposable domains, role accounts and likely typos; verdicts are valid (≥80 with MX present), risky (50–79, or a likely typo) and invalid.

0–100 的域名风险分。从 100 起扣：无 MX、未知域名、一次性域名、角色账号、疑似拼写错误都会扣分；裁决为 valid（≥80 且有 MX）、risky（50–79，或疑似拼错）、invalid。

How many addresses can I validate in one batch call?一次批量调用最多能验多少条？

Up to 100. A bad entry marks only itself invalid; the batch itself still succeeds, and the summary reports counts and percentages per verdict.

最多 100 条。坏条目只把自己标成 invalid，整批照常成功，summary 会给出各裁决的计数与百分比。
