How accurate is bulk email verification?批量邮箱验证到底有多准？

SHORT ANSWER短答

Per address, exactly as accurate as a single check — the checks are identical. What changes in bulk is the mix: on a real B2B list, roughly 30–40% of addresses usually sit on catch-all domains, where no verifier can give a definite yes or no. So the honest output of a bulk run is a segmented list — valid, risky and invalid, each row scored — not one accuracy percentage. Measure accuracy on your own list: send to the valid segment first and compare hard bounces band by band.

逐个地址看，和单查一样准——跑的检查完全相同。批量时变的只是名单构成：真实 B2B 名单里通常有约 30–40% 的地址在 catch-all 域名上，那部分任何验证工具都给不出确定答案。所以一次批量跑诚实的产出是一份分好段的名单——valid、risky、invalid，每行带分数——而不是一个准确率百分比。准确率要到你自己的名单上量：先发 valid 段，再按分数段对比硬退信。

Why a bulk run has no single accuracy number为什么批量跑没有单一准确率

Single-check benchmarks already cap out lower than the marketing suggests: in Hunter's public benchmark — 3,000 real business emails run through 15 verification services — the best service scored 70%. A bulk run inherits that per-address ceiling and adds a second problem: composition. Two lists run through the same verifier will report different "accuracy" purely because one has more catch-all domains, more role accounts or more typos in it. The verifier didn't change; the list did.

That is why a single percentage for a bulk job is the wrong unit. The useful output is the distribution: how many rows came back valid, how many risky and why (catch-all risk, disposable domain, a typo suggestion), and how many invalid. Those bands are what you actually act on.

单查的基准本来就比营销话术低：在 Hunter 的公开基准里——3,000 封真实商务邮箱、跑 15 家验证服务——最好的一家也只有 70%。批量跑继承这个逐地址天花板，还多一个问题：构成。两份名单跑同一个验证工具，报出来的「准确率」会不同，纯粹因为其中一份 catch-all 域名更多、角色账号更多或拼写错误更多。变的不是工具，是名单。

所以给一次批量任务报单一百分比是用错了单位。有用的产出是分布：多少行 valid、多少行 risky 以及原因（catch-all 风险、一次性域名、拼写纠正建议）、多少行 invalid。这些分段才是你真正要动手处理的东西。

How to measure accuracy on your own list怎么在自己的名单上量准确率

  • Seed it first. Add a few addresses you already know are good and a few you know are dead. If the verifier mislabels the seeds, stop there.
  • Keep the bands separate. Don't merge risky back into valid to make the list look bigger — the bands are the measurement.
  • Send in order. Mail the valid band first, the risky band later and separately, and never mail the invalid band.
  • Compare hard bounces per band. After the send, line the bounce rate up against the score bands. A verifier is accurate *for your list* when the bands predict the bounces: near-zero hard bounces in valid, visibly more in risky.
  • 先埋种子。放几个你已知是好的地址、几个已知是死的。验证工具把种子都标错，就不用往下走了。
  • 分段别合并。不要为了让名单看起来大，把 risky 并回 valid——分段本身就是测量。
  • 按顺序发。先发 valid 段，risky 段晚些单独发，invalid 段永远不发。
  • 按段对比硬退信。发完之后，把退信率和分数段对齐。对你的名单来说准的验证工具，应该是分段能预测退信：valid 段硬退信接近零，risky 段明显更多。

What EmailCheck returns per rowEmailCheck 逐行返回什么

Every row gets the same domain-level checks as a single lookup — format, domain and MX health, disposable and role-account signals, a typo suggestion when one is likely, domain age, and catch-all risk — combined into a 0–100 score and a valid / risky / invalid verdict, with the fired signals named. Rows whose signals run out — typically catch-all domains — come back scored as risky, not silently passed as valid.
