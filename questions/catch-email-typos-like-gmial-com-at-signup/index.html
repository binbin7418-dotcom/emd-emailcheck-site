How do I catch email typos like gmial.com at signup?怎么在注册环节抓住 gmial.com 这类邮箱拼写错误？

SHORT ANSWER短答

Check the domain, not just the format. When an address's domain looks like a misspelling of a known provider, EmailCheck returns typoLikely: true and a suggestion — gmial.com becomes gmail.com. Put that check in your signup form and ask “did you mean …?” before the account is created: recovering a mistyped address at signup is far cheaper than a bounced confirmation email and a lost user.

查域名，不只是查格式。当地址的域名看起来像某个已知服务商的拼写错误时，EmailCheck 返回 typoLikely: true 和一个 suggestion——gmial.com 变回 gmail.com。把这个检查放进注册表单，在账号创建前问一句「你是不是指……？」：在注册环节救回一个拼错的地址，比事后一封退信的确认邮件加一个流失的用户便宜得多。

Why typos sail through format checks为什么拼写错误能穿过格式检查

test@gmial.com is a perfectly well-formed email address: valid syntax, and the domain itself exists. What is broken is invisible to a regex — the user meant Gmail. Run it in the box on this page and look at the live result: score 40, verdict invalid (the typo domain has no MX records), with suggestion: test@gmail.com attached. Two facts did the work: the domain is one edit away from a major provider, and it cannot actually receive mail.

The cost of missing this at signup is paid later and in silence: the confirmation email goes nowhere, the user never activates, and support eventually hears “your emails never arrive” — if the user bothers at all.

test@gmial.com 是一个格式完全合法的邮箱地址：语法没错，域名本身也存在。坏掉的地方正则看不见——用户想写的是 Gmail。用本页的框跑它，看生产结果：40 分、裁决 invalid（这个拼错域名没有 MX 记录），并附 suggestion: test@gmail.com。起作用的是两个事实：该域名与主流服务商只差一次编辑距离；且它实际上收不了信。

在注册环节漏掉它的代价是事后默默付的：确认邮件无处可去，用户永远不激活，客服最终听到「你们的邮件从来没到过」——如果用户还愿意说的话。

The form pattern that fixes it能修好它的表单模式

  1. On submit (or on blur of the email field), call GET /validate?email=... — it is one fast, local-computation check.
  2. If typoLikely is true, show the suggestion inline: “Did you mean test@gmail.com?” with one-tap accept. Never rewrite the address silently — the suggestion can occasionally be wrong, and the user owns their address.
  3. If the verdict is invalid for other reasons (no MX, disposable domain), say so specifically rather than with a generic “invalid email” — the response tells you which check failed.
  4. Log the correction rate. Teams are routinely surprised how much of their “bad email” problem is this one pattern.
  1. 在提交时（或邮箱字段失焦时）调 GET /validate?email=...——一次快速的本地计算检查。
  2. 如果 typoLikely 为真，内联显示建议：「你是不是指 test@gmail.com？」并给一键接受。永远不要静默改写地址——建议偶尔也会错，地址是用户的。
  3. 如果裁决因其他原因 invalid（无 MX、一次性域名），把具体原因说出来，而不是笼统一句「邮箱无效」——响应里写了是哪项检查没过。
  4. 记录纠正率。团队通常会惊讶：他们的「坏邮箱」问题有多大比例就是这一个模式。
