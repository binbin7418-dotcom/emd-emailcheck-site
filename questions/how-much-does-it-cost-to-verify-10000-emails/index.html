How much should it cost to verify 10,000 emails?验证 10,000 个邮箱应该花多少钱？

SHORT ANSWER短答

The market consensus is roughly $25–75 per 10,000 verifications — the range experienced buyers cite publicly, including in a highly-upvoted Reddit thread collected in our research. Higher prices do not buy higher accuracy: the best independently measured service in the category scored 70%. EmailCheck's PRO plan is $9 per 10,000 requests, and the free BASIC tier (100 checks a month) exists so you can test before paying anything.

市场共识价约为 $25–75/10,000 次——这是有经验的买家公开给出的区间，包括我们调研里收录的一个 Reddit 高赞帖。价格高不等于更准：品类里独立实测最好的一家也只有 70%。EmailCheck 的 PRO 档是 $9/10,000 次，另有免费 BASIC 档（每月 100 次），可以先试再付钱。

Where the $25–75 range comes from$25–75 这个区间的来历

The range is what buyers report paying, not what vendors advertise: it comes from practitioner discussions of per-10K pricing across the well-known services. What pushes a quote toward the top of the range is usually bundling — claimed SMTP probing, catch-all “resolution”, enrichment data — rather than measurably better verdicts. Given the 70% independent accuracy ceiling, paying triple for the same list buys you a bigger invoice, not a cleaner list.

Watch the meter, too: per-submission billing means unresolved (unknown / catch-all) results consume the same credits as verdicts at most vendors, which raises your effective cost per usable answer well above the sticker rate.

这个区间是买家报告的实际支出，不是厂商的广告价：来自从业者对各家知名服务按万次计价的讨论。把报价推向区间上沿的通常是捆绑——声称的 SMTP 探测、catch-all「解决」、数据补全——而不是可测量地更好的裁决。鉴于独立实测的准确率天花板是 70%，为同一份名单付三倍价钱，买到的是更厚的账单，不是更干净的名单。

还要看计费方式：按提交计费意味着未决结果（unknown / catch-all）在多数厂商那里和裁决扣一样的额度，你为「每个可用答案」付的实际价格会比标价高出不少。
