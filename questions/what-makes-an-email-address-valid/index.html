What makes an email address valid?什么样的邮箱地址算有效？

SHORT ANSWER短答

Validity is layered. An address is well-formed (one @, legal characters, a real-looking domain). Its domain exists and publishes MX records that route mail. And no disqualifying signal fires: the domain is not a disposable provider, and the address is not undermined by a likely typo. On top of that sit graded risks rather than failures — role accounts, free-provider domains and catch-all behaviour. EmailCheck folds the layers into a 0–100 score: valid means 80 or above with MX present and no risk signal fired. Valid is a statement about deliverability evidence, not a promise that a specific person will read the message — mailbox existence is a separate question no domain-level check can settle.

有效性是分层的。地址本身格式正确（一个 @、字符合法、域名像样）；域名存在、且发布了能路由邮件的 MX 记录；没有触发否决性信号：域名不是一次性服务商、地址没有疑似拼写问题。在这之上是分级风险而非失败——角色账号、免费服务商域名、catch-all 行为。EmailCheck 把这些层合成 0–100 分：valid 指 80 分以上、有 MX、且没有风险信号触发。Valid 说的是投递证据，不是「某个具体的人会读这封信」的承诺——邮箱存在是另一个问题，域名级检查解决不了。

The layers, in order按顺序看这几层

Syntax. The address must parse: exactly one @ separating local part and domain, no spaces or illegal characters, a domain that contains a dot and a real top-level domain. This layer alone rejects a surprising share of form submissions. Domain. The domain must exist in DNS — a registered, resolvable name, not a string someone invented. Routing. The domain must publish MX records; without them there is nowhere for mail to go, however perfect the address looks. Signals. Only then do the graded checks apply: disposable provider, role account, free provider, typo pattern, domain age, catch-all risk. An address is “valid” when it clears the first three layers and none of the signals is disqualifying.

格式。地址必须能解析：恰好一个 @ 分隔本地部分和域名、没有空格或非法字符、域名带点且顶级域名真实。光这一层就能拒掉相当比例的表单提交。域名。域名必须在 DNS 里存在——是注册过、能解析的名字，不是谁编的字符串。路由。域名必须发布 MX 记录；没有 MX，信无处可去，地址长得再完美也没用。信号。到这一步才轮到分级检查：一次性服务商、角色账号、免费服务商、拼写模式、域名年龄、catch-all 风险。一个地址「有效」，是它过了前三层、且没有任何信号构成否决。

Valid, risky, invalid: where the lines areValid、risky、invalid：线画在哪

The bands turn the layers into decisions. Valid (80–100): every layer passed, no risk signal fired — send. Risky (50–79): the domain routes mail but something needs a decision — a catch-all risk you cannot resolve further, a disposable domain you may still choose to mail once, a typo you should correct first. Invalid (below 50): a hard failure — broken format, nonexistent domain, no MX, or a likely typo with no working MX behind it. The risky band is the honest middle that binary valid/invalid tools hide: on real lists it is a large share of addresses, and what you do with it (see what a risky verdict means) is most of list hygiene.

分段把这几层变成决策。Valid（80–100）：每层都过、没有风险信号触发——发。Risky（50–79）：域名能收信，但有需要你拿主意的东西——无法再确认的 catch-all 风险、你可能仍选择发一次的一次性域名、应该先纠正的拼写。Invalid（50 以下）：硬失败——格式坏了、域名不存在、没有 MX、或疑似拼错且背后没有可用 MX。Risky 段是二元 valid/invalid 工具藏起来的诚实中间地带：在真实名单里它占很大比例，怎么处置它（见risky 裁决是什么意思）就是名单清洗的大部分工作。

What valid does not promiseValid 不承诺什么

A valid verdict does not say the mailbox exists — a domain can route mail perfectly while the individual account was deleted last week, and on catch-all domains every name is “accepted” by design. It does not say the person wants your mail; consent and engagement are your list's history, not a property of the address. And it does not freeze time: domains lapse and accounts close, which is why a list verified once still needs re-verification before later sends. What valid does say is narrower and, for sending decisions, sufficient: the address is real in every way that can be checked without sending, and nothing about it predicts a bounce. Valid 裁决不表示邮箱存在——域名可以完美地路由邮件，而具体账号上周刚被删掉；在 catch-all 域名上，每个名字按设计都会被「接受」。它也不表示对方想收你的信；同意和互动是你名单的历史，不是地址的属性。它更不冻结时间：域名会过期、账号会关闭，所以验过一次的名单在后续发送前仍要重验。Valid 真正说的更窄，但对发送决策已经足够：这个地址在一切「不发信可查」的意义上都是真的，没有任何一点预示退信。把任何地址丢进验证邮箱地址检查器，就能逐层看它自己的结果。
