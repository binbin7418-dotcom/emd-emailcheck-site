Why are a third of the addresses on my B2B list on catch-all domains?为什么我 B2B 名单里三分之一的地址都在 catch-all 域名上？

SHORT ANSWER短答

Because many companies configure their domain to accept mail for every possible address — it guarantees no legitimate message is ever turned away at the server. Public data puts 30–40% of a typical B2B list on catch-all domains. That slice of your list cannot be “verified clean”; the workable approach is to segment it and rank it by risk signals instead of treating it like the rest of the list.

因为很多公司把自己的域名配置成：任何地址都收信——这样就保证任何一封正经邮件都不会在服务器门口被拒。公开数据里，典型 B2B 名单有 30–40% 的地址在 catch-all 域名上。名单的这一片没法「验干净」；可行的办法是把它分出来、用风险信号排序，而不是和名单其余部分一视同仁。

Why companies set their domains up this way公司为什么这样配自己的域名

Catch-all is a rational default for a business. Employees join and leave, people guess at addresses (firstname@, firstinitial-lastname@), and a rejected message can mean a lost lead that nobody ever hears about. Accepting everything and filtering afterwards trades a spam problem the company can manage for a silence problem it cannot. Small and mid-size companies — the bulk of most B2B lists — choose this most often, which is why the share is so high.

对企业来说，catch-all 是理性默认。员工来来去去，外人会猜地址格式（firstname@、首字母-lastname@），一封被拒的邮件可能意味着一条谁都不知道的线索就丢了。全收、事后再过滤，等于用一个可管理的垃圾邮件问题，换掉一个管不了的沉默问题。中小公司——多数 B2B 名单的主体——最常这样选，所以占比才这么高。

What it means for your list这对你的名单意味着什么

  • Catch-all domains call for a signal, not a stamp. EmailCheck scores that third of your list with a catch-all risk signal, so you can segment it and decide per segment instead of treating every address as confirmed.
  • Don't delete them. A third of a B2B list contains real customers at real companies. Deleting on “unverifiable” throws away pipeline.
  • Don't blend them either. Mail catch-all addresses as their own segment, in smaller batches, with bounce monitoring, so a bad pocket cannot damage the sender reputation your verified segment relies on.
  • Rank by the signals you have. MX health, domain age, typo and disposable flags all still work on catch-all domains — EmailCheck returns them per address, with catchAllRisk marking the segment, and the batch endpoint's summary lets you see the segment's size before you send.
  • Catch-all 域名要的是信号，不是印章。EmailCheck 给这三分之一名单打上 catch-all 风险信号，你可以分段处理、逐段决定，而不是把每个地址都当成已确认。
  • 不要删掉它们。B2B 名单的三分之一里是真公司里的真客户。因「不可验证」删掉，等于扔掉管线。
  • 也不要混着发。把 catch-all 地址当作独立分区分开发、小批量、盯退信，让坏口袋伤不到你已验证分区依赖的发信声誉。
  • 用你有的信号排序。MX 健康度、域名年龄、拼写与一次性标记在 catch-all 域名上照样有效——EmailCheck 逐条返回，并用 catchAllRisk 标出这个分区；批量端点的 summary 让你在发送前就看到这个分区的大小。
