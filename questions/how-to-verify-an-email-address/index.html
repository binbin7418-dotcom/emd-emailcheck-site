How do I verify an email address?怎么验证一个邮箱地址？

SHORT ANSWER短答

Three ways, in order of effort. Fastest: paste the address into a checker and read the verdict — you get format, domain and MX checks, disposable and role-account flags, a typo suggestion and a catch-all risk signal in one result. By hand: confirm the address is well-formed, then look up the domain's MX records (for example with nslookup or dig) to see whether it accepts mail at all. In code: call a verification API at the moment the address is captured, so typos are caught while the person can still fix them. All three are domain-level: they establish that the address is well-formed and deliverable in principle — none of them proves a specific mailbox exists.

三种办法，按费力程度排。最快：把地址粘进检查器读裁决——格式、域名与 MX 检查、一次性与角色账号标记、拼写建议、catch-all 风险信号，一次全拿到。手工：先确认地址格式正确，再查域名的 MX 记录（比如用 nslookup 或 dig）看它到底收不收信。写代码：在地址被收集的当下调验证 API，让拼写错误在人还能改的时候就被抓住。三种都是域名级：确认的是地址格式正确、原则上可投递——都不证明某个具体邮箱存在。

The manual way: what you can check yourself手工办法：你自己能查什么

Two of the layers are checkable by hand. Format: exactly one @, no spaces, a plausible local part and a domain with a dot. Routing: query the domain's MX records — on a command line, nslookup -type=mx gmail.com or dig MX gmail.com; no MX records means the domain cannot receive mail, whatever the address looks like. What you cannot easily do by hand is the signal layer: knowing which of the thousands of disposable providers a domain belongs to, recognising typo patterns against major providers, reading domain age from registration records, or estimating catch-all behaviour. That layer is why checkers exist.

有两层可以手工查。格式：恰好一个 @、没有空格、本地部分合理、域名带点。路由：查域名的 MX 记录——命令行里 nslookup -type=mx gmail.com 或 dig MX gmail.com；没有 MX 记录，这个域名就收不了信，地址长得再像也没用。手工做不了的是信号层：认出某个域名属于成千上万个一次性服务商中的哪一家、对照主流服务商识别拼写模式、从注册记录读域名年龄、估计 catch-all 行为。这一层正是检查器存在的理由。

Verifying in code, at capture在代码里、收集当下验证

If addresses arrive through your own form, the highest-value moment to verify is during signup, not in a cleanup run weeks later: a typo suggestion shown to the person typing converts a lost user into a corrected address. That is what the API form of the same check is for — one call per signup, verdict and suggestion returned in the response. The pattern is covered in how to verify emails at signup with an API. For a list you already hold, switch to the bulk email checker instead of looping single checks.

如果地址是从你自己的表单进来的，验证价值最高的时刻是注册当下，而不是几周后的清洗轮：给正在输入的人看拼写建议，能把一个要丢掉的用户变成一个被纠正的地址。同一个检查的 API 形态就是干这个的——每次注册调一次，裁决和建议在响应里返回。具体做法见怎么在注册时用 API 验证邮箱。手上已经是一份名单的话，换批量检查器，不要循环单查。
