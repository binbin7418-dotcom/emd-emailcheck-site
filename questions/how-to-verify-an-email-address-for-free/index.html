How can I verify an email address for free?怎么免费验证一个邮箱地址？

SHORT ANSWER短答

You have three genuinely free routes. Web checkers like the one on this site verify a single address with no sign-up and no charge. Free API tiers — EmailCheck's BASIC plan includes 1,000 checks a month — cover low-volume pipelines. And the manual route costs nothing but time: check the format yourself and look up the domain's MX records with nslookup or dig. What free tiers normally limit is volume and support, not the check itself: the same engine runs behind the free and paid plans here. What to avoid is “free” tools that ask you to upload a whole list to an unknown service — a customer list is worth more than the check.

真正免费的路有三条。本站这样的网页检查器：单个地址、不用注册、不收费。免费 API 档——EmailCheck 的 BASIC 档含每月 1,000 次——够低量管线用。手工路线只花时间：自己看格式，再用 nslookup 或 dig 查域名的 MX 记录。免费档通常限制的是量和支持，不是检查本身：本站免费和付费跑的是同一个引擎。要避开的是让你把整份名单上传到来路不明服务的「免费」工具——一份客户名单比一次检查值钱得多。

What free usually limits — and what it should not免费通常限制什么——以及不该限制什么

Legitimate free tiers meter quantity: calls per month, list size per run, sometimes support response time. The check itself should be identical — a free result produced by a weaker engine is a demo, not a tier. Hold any tool (including this one) to that standard: run the same awkward addresses through the free route and a paid route if you can, and compare. Where free genuinely runs out is scale and certainty of supply: a pipeline verifying 50,000 addresses a month needs a paid quota, and a business process that depends on the check needs a plan where the limits are contractual rather than courtesy.

正经的免费档计量的是数量：每月调用数、每轮名单大小，有时还有支持响应时间。检查本身应该一模一样——免费结果若来自更弱的引擎，那是演示，不是档位。拿这个标准要求任何工具（包括本站）：把同一批别扭地址分别走免费和付费路线，比一比。免费真正到头的地方是规模和供给确定性：每月验 50,000 条的管线需要付费额度，业务流程依赖这个检查的，需要一份限额写进合同而不是靠善意的套餐。

The privacy catch with free web tools免费网页工具的隐私坑

A single address sent to a checker reveals little. A full customer list uploaded to an unknown “free bulk verifier” reveals your market: who your customers are, how many you have, and when you clean your data. Free tools monetise somehow, and an uploaded list is an asset. Prefer tools that state what happens to submissions — on this site, addresses are sent over HTTPS to the API and returned with their results; they are not added to any mailing list, and the bulk page keeps no copy of your file (it is read in your browser). If a free tool will not say what it does with your list, price the check accordingly: it is not free.

发给检查器的单个地址，泄露不了多少。把整份客户名单上传给来路不明的「免费批量验证」，泄露的是你的市场：客户是谁、有多少、你什么时候洗数据。免费工具总要变现，而上传的名单就是资产。优先选把提交物去向说清的工具——本站里，地址经 HTTPS 发到 API、带着结果返回；不会被加进任何邮寄名单，批量页也不留你的文件副本（文件在你的浏览器里读）。一个免费工具如果不肯说它拿你的名单干什么，就按真实价格给这次检查估值：它不是免费的。
