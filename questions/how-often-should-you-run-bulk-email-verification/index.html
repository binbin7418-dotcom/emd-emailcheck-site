How often should you run bulk email verification?应该多久跑一次批量邮箱验证？

SHORT ANSWER短答

Run it on triggers, not just on a calendar. The non-negotiable run is before any significant send to a list that has been sitting: addresses decay as people change jobs, companies fold or rebrand, and domains lapse, so a list that was clean six months ago is not clean now. Beyond that: verify every import before it merges into your main list, re-verify any segment you have not mailed in a few months, and run a full pass after any send whose bounce rate surprised you. Addresses captured through a form should be checked singly, at capture — bulk runs are for the list you already hold.

按触发条件跑，不只看日历。不可省的一轮，是给一份放了一段时间的名单做重要发送之前：人会换工作、公司会倒闭或改名、域名会过期，半年前干净的名单现在不干净。此外：每次导入在并入主名单之前先验；几个月没发过的分段，发送前重验；任何一次退信率出乎意料的发送之后，整份跑一遍找原因。通过表单收集的地址应该在收集当下逐条查——批量跑管的是你已经持有的名单。

Why lists go stale名单为什么会变质

An email address is a claim about a person at a company on a domain, and all three parts move. People change jobs and their work addresses are retired. Companies get acquired, rebrand and let old domains lapse — when the domain goes, every address on it goes. Providers shut down or get absorbed. And capture never stops adding noise: every form submission is a chance for a fresh typo. B2B lists are commonly estimated to decay at roughly a fifth to a third of their addresses per year; your own list's rate depends on how B2B and how old it is, but the direction is guaranteed. Verification does not wear out — the list does.

一个邮箱地址，是对「某个人、在某家公司、用某个域名」的断言，而这三部分都会动。人换工作，工作邮箱就被注销；公司被收购、改名，旧域名过期——域名一没，上面所有地址全没；服务商关停或被合并。而收集端从不停地加噪：每次表单提交都可能带来一个新的拼写错误。业内普遍估计 B2B 名单每年约有五分之一到三分之一的地址失效；你自己名单的速率取决于 B2B 程度和年龄，但方向是注定的。过期的不是验证，是名单。

The triggers that matter真正要紧的触发条件

  • Before a big send to an old list. The run is cheap; a bounce spike costs sender reputation that takes weeks to earn back.
  • At import time. Verify before the merge, not after — once bad rows are inside your main list, they are indistinguishable from your own data.
  • Before re-activating a dormant segment. A segment untouched for three to six months gets a fresh run, even if it was verified when collected.
  • After a bad send. If hard bounces jumped, verify the list and compare verdict bands against the bounces — that tells you whether the problem is list rot or something upstream.
  • 给老名单做大发送之前。跑一轮很便宜；退信尖峰伤的是发信声誉，那要几周才能养回来。
  • 导入的那一刻。在并入之前验，不是之后——坏行一旦进了主名单，就和你自己的数据分不开了。
  • 重新启用休眠分段之前。三到六个月没碰过的分段，重新跑一轮，哪怕收集时验过。
  • 一次坏发送之后。硬退信跳了，就把名单验一遍、把裁决分段和退信对上——这样能分清是名单腐烂还是上游问题。

A workable cadence一套能落地的节奏

For an actively mailed list: single checks at capture (the API or the form-side check), a bulk run before every major campaign, and a full-list pass roughly quarterly to catch what campaign-by-campaign runs miss. For a list you mail rarely, the calendar matters less than the rule “never send to a segment that has not been verified since it last changed or in the last few months”. Each run also gets cheaper to act on than the last: typo corrections and dead domains you remove stay removed. The bulk email checker covers the pre-send run free for the first 100 addresses at a time.

对一份在持续发送的名单：收集当下逐条查（API 或表单侧检查），每个大活动之前跑一轮批量，每季度左右整份过一遍，补上逐活动跑漏掉的部分。对很少发送的名单，日历不重要，规则重要：「从没在最近变动之后、或最近几个月内验过的分段，不发」。而且每一轮都比上一轮好处理：你删掉的拼写纠正和死域名，不会再回来。批量检查器免费覆盖发送前这一轮，每次前 100 条。
