How do I verify emails at signup with an API?怎么在注册环节用 API 验证邮箱？

SHORT ANSWER短答

Call a validation endpoint once, when the form is submitted, and act on the verdict in three bands. Block the hard-invalid: bad syntax, dead domains, no MX records — those accounts can never receive a confirmation email anyway. Warn, don't block, on the fixable: a likely typo (the API returns a suggestion like gmail.com for gmial.com) and disposable addresses, where your policy decides. Let everything else through, including catch-all domains, flagged with the risk signal for later segmentation. With EmailCheck that is one GET or POST to /validate per signup; the free tier covers 100 checks a month for wiring and testing.

在表单提交时调一次验证接口，按三档处理裁决。硬无效直接拦：格式错误、死域名、没有 MX——这类账号本来也收不到确认邮件。可修的只警告、不拦截：大概率拼写错误（接口会返回建议，如 gmial.com → gmail.com）和一次性地址，后者拦不拦由你的产品政策定。其余全部放行，包括 catch-all 域名，只带上风险信号供事后分区。用 EmailCheck 就是每次注册对 /validate 发一个 GET 或 POST；免费档每月 100 次，够接线和测试。

The three bands: block, warn, allow三档处理：拦、警告、放行

Most signup-verification bugs come from treating the verdict as binary. A validation result has at least three bands, and each deserves a different UX:

  • Block — hard invalid. Syntax errors, domains that do not exist, domains with no mail server. There is no account-recovery or confirmation path for these addresses; accepting them creates a dead account and a guaranteed bounce. Show a plain error and let the user correct the field.
  • Warn — fixable or policy cases. A typo suggestion (“Did you mean gmail.com?”) converts a lost signup into a good one with a single click — never silently autocorrect, always confirm with the user. Disposable addresses are a policy call: a free-trial product may block them, a newsletter may merely flag them.
  • Allow — everything else, flagged. Valid domains, role accounts (support@, admin@) if your product allows them, and catch-all domains with their catchAllRisk signal stored on the account for later segmentation — not silently treated as proven.

注册验证的大多数 bug 来自把裁决当二值。一次验证结果至少有三档，每档配不同的交互：

  • 拦——硬无效。格式错误、域名不存在、域名没有邮件服务器。这类地址没有找回账号和确认邮件的任何路径；收下来就是一个死账号加一次必定的退信。给出明确报错，让用户改字段。
  • 警告——可修或看政策。拼写建议（「你是不是想填 gmail.com？」）一次点击就能把流失的注册救回来——绝不静默自动纠正，永远让用户确认。一次性地址看政策：免费试用产品可以拦，订阅通讯可以只标记。
  • 放行——其余全部，带标记。有效域名、你的产品允许的角色账号（support@、admin@），以及 catch-all 域名——把它的 catchAllRisk 信号存到账号上供事后分区，不要静默当作已证实。

Where to put the call (and where not to)调用放哪儿（以及不放哪儿）

Call on submit, not on every keystroke: one check per attempted signup is enough, keeps you inside free-tier limits while testing, and avoids hammering the API from half-typed addresses. Debounce-and-check on blur is acceptable for the typo suggestion alone, but the blocking decision belongs at submit, with the full address final.

Fail open, deliberately: if the validation call times out or errors, let the signup through and log it for a later batch re-check. A verification outage must never become your outage — but “fail open” should be a written policy with a re-check queue, not an accident you discover from bounce reports. And never block on mailbox existence: no API can check it at domain level (see the linked page on what verification actually checks), so do not design a step that pretends to.

在提交时调，不要每次按键都调：每次尝试注册查一次就够，测试期也压得住免费档额度，还不会拿打了一半的地址去轰接口。只为拼写建议在失焦时查一次也可以，但拦截决策必须放在提交时、用最终完整地址。

要有意地「失败放行」：验证调用超时或报错时，让注册通过，并把地址记进事后批量复查队列。验证服务故障绝不能变成你的故障——但「失败放行」应该是写下来的政策加复查队列，而不是等退信报告来了才发现的事故。另外，绝不要拿邮箱存在性做拦截点：域名级检查查不了这个（见链接页对验证到底查什么的说明），不要设计一个假装能查的环节。
