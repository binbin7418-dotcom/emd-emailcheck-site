Can a catch-all email address actually be verified?Catch-all 邮箱地址真的能被验证吗？

SHORT ANSWER短答

A catch-all domain accepts mail for every address, so a hard yes/no verdict is the wrong output for it — the useful output is a risk signal. EmailCheck scores catch-all addresses as risky with the reason attached, so you can segment them: keep, warm up separately, or drop. Even the biggest vendors barely move this number: published benchmark figures put catch-all “resolution” at about 12% for ZeroBounce, 8% for NeverBounce and 5% for MillionVerifier.

Catch-all 域名对一切地址都收信，所以对它下硬性的对/错裁决是错的输出——有用的输出是风险信号。EmailCheck 把 catch-all 地址评为 risky 并附原因，方便你分层处理：保留、单独预热、或剔除。就连最大的厂商也撬不动这个数字：公开基准数据里，catch-all 的「解决率」约为 ZeroBounce 12%、NeverBounce 8%、MillionVerifier 5%。

What “catch-all” actually means「Catch-all」到底是什么意思

A catch-all (accept-all) mail server is configured to accept messages for any local part at the domain — real mailboxes, former employees, addresses that never existed. From the outside, a probe gets the same “accepted” answer for all of them, which is why EmailCheck scores them with a catch-all risk signal instead of a plain valid stamp.

This is a deliberate, legitimate configuration, common at companies that would rather receive a mis-addressed message than lose it. Roughly 30–40% of the addresses on a typical B2B list sit on catch-all domains, so this is not an edge case — it is a third of your list.

Catch-all（全收）邮件服务器被配置成：域名下任何本地名都收信——真邮箱、前员工、从来不存在的地址，一视同仁。从外面探测，得到的永远是同一个「接受」回答，所以 EmailCheck 给它们打的是 catch-all 风险信号，而不是一个光秃秃的 valid 印章。

这是有意为之的合法配置，常见于「宁可收错一封、不可漏一封」的公司。典型 B2B 名单里约 30–40% 的地址落在 catch-all 域名上——这不是边角情况，是你名单的三分之一。

What vendors who claim to “verify” catch-all are doing声称能「验证」catch-all 的厂商在做什么

When a tool shows a confident verdict for a catch-all address, it is scoring: domain age, provider patterns, engagement data from its own sending network. Some of that scoring is useful. The published resolution rates — 12%, 8%, 5% for the three best-known vendors — tell you how much certainty is actually available: a graded signal is the useful product, and it is what EmailCheck returns in the catchAllRisk field with every response.

当一个工具对 catch-all 地址给出自信裁决时，它做的是打分：域名年龄、服务商模式、它自己发送网络里的互动数据。这类打分有些是有用的。公开的解决率——三家最知名厂商 12%、8%、5%——说明真实能拿到的确定性就这么多：分级信号才是有用的产品形态，EmailCheck 在每个响应的 catchAllRisk 字段里返回的正是这个。

What to do with catch-all addressesCatch-all 地址该怎么处理

Keep them, but keep them apart. Segment catch-all addresses out of your main list, rank them by the risk signals you do have (domain age, MX health, whether the address pattern matches a real person's name format), and mail them in small batches with bounce monitoring. The mistake is binary thinking — deleting a third of a B2B list throws away real customers, and mailing it blind risks your sender reputation.

留着，但分开。把 catch-all 地址从主名单里分出来，用你手里有的风险信号排序（域名年龄、MX 健康度、地址格式像不像真人姓名格式），小批量发送并盯退信。二元思维才是错误：删掉 B2B 名单的三分之一等于扔掉真实客户，闭眼群发则拿发信声誉冒险。
