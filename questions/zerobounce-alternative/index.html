First: two different products share one name先分清：同名的其实是两种产品

“Email verification” covers two different operations, and comparison pages that blur them waste your money:

  • SMTP probing (ZeroBounce, NeverBounce, MillionVerifier, Bouncer). The verifier opens a conversation with the receiving mail server and asks about the specific address. That is why these services cost more per check — and why catch-all domains still come back “unknown”: a catch-all server accepts everything, so every address gets the same answer.
  • EmailCheck's checks. Syntax, whether the domain exists and publishes MX records, SPF/DMARC/DKIM posture, disposable-domain and role-account signals, likely typos with a concrete correction (gmial.com → gmail.com), domain age from registration records, and a graded catch-all risk signal. It is fast and cheap, and a “valid” verdict means every check passed: the address is well-formed, the domain is real and routable, and no risk signal fired.

Neither is “better” in the abstract. The question is which conclusion your workflow acts on: a signup form acts on typos, disposable addresses and dead domains — all answerable in one fast call, needed in milliseconds — while list cleaning before a big send acts on per-mailbox signals.

「邮箱验证」这个名字底下其实是两种活，把两者混为一谈的对比页只会让你多花钱：

  • SMTP 探测（ZeroBounce、NeverBounce、MillionVerifier、Bouncer）。验证方与收件服务器对话，逐个地址询问。代价是单价更高，而且 catch-all 域名照样只能返回「unknown」：catch-all 服务器什么都收，每个地址得到的都是同一个回答。
  • EmailCheck 的检查。格式、域名是否存在并公布 MX 记录、SPF/DMARC/DKIM 配置、一次性域名与角色账号信号、带具体纠正的拼写提示（gmial.com → gmail.com）、来自注册记录的域名年龄，以及分级的 catch-all 风险信号。它快、便宜，「valid」表示各项检查全部通过：地址格式正确、域名真实可投递、没有风险信号触发。

抽象地说没有谁更好。关键看你的流程依据哪种结论行动：注册表单要拦的是拼写错误、一次性地址和死域名——一次快速调用就能答，还得在毫秒内给出；大发送前的名单清洗靠的则是另一类逐邮箱信号。

When ZeroBounce — or a rival — is the better choice什么时候 ZeroBounce（或它的对手）才是对的选择

Buy a probing verifier when the conclusion you need is about a mailbox, not a domain:

  • Cleaning a purchased or inherited list before a large send. Mailbox-level signals are the product there.
  • Very large bulk runs. At 100,000+ addresses, MillionVerifier's per-check price undercuts every subscription on this page — probing included.
  • One vendor for verification plus adjacent tooling. ZeroBounce ONE bundles verification with DMARC monitoring and deliverability tools; if you will actually use the bundle, the subscription math changes.

And buy none of them — EmailCheck included — expecting certainty on catch-all domains. On a catch-all server every verifier is reduced to signals; anyone who sells you a definite answer there is selling the signal under a different label.

当你要的结论关乎邮箱、而不是域名时，买探测型：

  • 大发送前清洗买来的或继承的名单。那里的产品就是逐邮箱的信号。
  • 超大批量。10 万条以上，MillionVerifier 的单次价格比本页任何订阅都低——而且还含探测。
  • 一家搞定验证加周边工具。ZeroBounce ONE 把验证与 DMARC 监控、投递工具打包；你真会用上整包的话，订阅账要重算。

还有一条对所有家都成立（包括 EmailCheck）：别指望任何人在 catch-all 域名上给出确定答案。catch-all 服务器面前每个验证方都只剩信号，谁卖你确定结论，谁就是把信号换了个标签卖。
