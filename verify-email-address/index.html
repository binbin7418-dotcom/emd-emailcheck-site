SHORT ANSWER短答

To verify an email address, run it through the checks that decide whether mail can reach it: is it well-formed, does the domain exist and publish MX records, is the domain a disposable provider, is the address a role account, does the domain look like a typo of a major provider, how old is the domain, and how risky is its catch-all behaviour. Those checks combine into a 0–100 score and a valid / risky / invalid verdict. That is what the tool above does, live, for one address at a time. It verifies the domain behind the address — it does not probe mailboxes, and it never claims a specific mailbox exists.

验证一个邮箱地址，就是跑一遍决定邮件能不能到达的检查：格式是否正确、域名是否存在并发布了 MX 记录、域名是不是一次性服务商、地址是不是角色账号、域名像不像主流服务商的拼写错误、域名有多老、它的 catch-all 行为风险多大。这些检查合成 0–100 分和 valid / risky / invalid 裁决。上面的工具就是实时在做这件事，一次一个地址。它验证的是地址背后的域名——不探测邮箱，也从不声称某个具体邮箱存在。

What gets checked查什么

  • Syntax — the address is well-formed: one @, legal characters, a plausible domain.
  • Domain & MX — the domain exists and publishes MX records that route mail; no MX means nowhere for mail to go.
  • Disposable & role signals — throwaway providers (Mailinator and kin) and role accounts (admin@, support@) are flagged, not hidden.
  • Typo detection — gmial.com, hotmial.com, yahooo.com: the likely intended domain is named, with the corrected address.
  • Domain age — read from registration records; a domain registered last week scores differently from one registered in 2004.
  • Catch-all risk — domains that accept mail for every address get a graded risk signal, labelled as a signal — the only honest output, since such domains cannot be resolved further without sending mail.

Every result shows the checks individually, not just the headline verdict — what makes an email address valid walks through the layers in order, and how to verify an email address covers the manual and in-code routes too.

  • 格式——地址合法：一个 @、字符合法、域名像样。
  • 域名与 MX——域名存在、且发布了能路由邮件的 MX 记录；没有 MX，信就无处可去。
  • 一次性与角色信号——临时邮箱服务商（Mailinator 之类）和角色账号（admin@、support@）会被标记，不藏着。
  • 拼写检测——gmial.com、hotmial.com、yahooo.com：指出大概率想输的域名，并给出纠正后的地址。
  • 域名年龄——来自注册记录；上周刚注册的域名和 2004 年注册的域名，得分逻辑不同。
  • Catch-all 风险——对所有地址都收信的域名，给出分级风险信号并明示它是信号——这是唯一诚实的输出，因为这类域名不发信就无法再确认。

每个结果都逐项展示检查，而不只是一个总裁决——什么样的邮箱地址算有效按顺序讲了每一层，怎么验证一个邮箱地址还讲了手工和代码两种路线。
