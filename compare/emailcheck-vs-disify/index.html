EmailCheck vs DisifyEmailCheck 对比 Disify

Both APIs validate an email address at domain level — format, DNS/MX, disposable and role signals, typo suggestions — and neither one probes mailboxes over SMTP. They differ in shape. Disify gives you far more free volume (30,000 validations a month on a free account) and paid plans with no monthly cap at all. EmailCheck gives you a decision in every response: a 0–100 score, a valid / risky / invalid verdict, a catch-all risk signal and the domain's age — fields Disify either doesn't return or reserves for its Pro plan. If you verify at signup and act on a verdict, that output is the product. If you verify in bulk and read raw signals, Disify's volume is hard to beat.

两家都是域名级验证——格式、DNS/MX、一次性与角色信号、拼写建议——都不做 SMTP 邮箱探测。差别在形态。Disify 免费量大得多（免费账户每月 30,000 次），付费档干脆不限月量。EmailCheck 给的是每个响应里都有一个可直接用的结论：0–100 分、valid / risky / invalid 裁决、catch-all 风险信号和域名年龄——这些字段 Disify 要么不返回、要么只在 Pro 档给。在注册口验证、按裁决行动，买的就是这个输出；大批量验证、自己读原始信号，Disify 的量很难被超过。

Same input, both APIs (measured 2026-11-24)同一输入，两家实测（2026-11-24）

We called both APIs with the same three addresses on 2026-11-24 — Disify anonymously, EmailCheck on its production endpoint — three times each for the typo address. The verdicts agreed every time; the shape of the answers did not.

  • test@gmial.com (a typo of gmail.com). Both caught it and both suggested gmail.com. Disify also flags gmial.com itself as disposable (it sits on their blacklist) and returned confidence: 100. EmailCheck returned verdict invalid, score 40, the corrected full address test@gmail.com, the domain's age (8,596 days) and catch-all risk unknown.
  • someone@gmail.com. EmailCheck: score 100, verdict valid, catch-all risk low. Disify: all flags clean (dns true, disposable false, free true) — and its confidence field read 0 for this address, against 100 for both bad ones above and below.
  • user@mailinator.com (disposable). Both flagged it as disposable. EmailCheck: verdict risky, score 50, catch-all risk possible. Disify: disposable: true with blacklist signals.

One honest note in EmailCheck's favour and one against. In its favour: the score and verdict are computed for you, on every tier. Against it: on mailinator it still says valid: true at the top level alongside the risky verdict — read the verdict and the score, not the boolean.

2026-11-24 我们用同三个地址调了两家 API——Disify 匿名调、EmailCheck 调生产端点——拼写地址各跑了 3 次。每次裁决都一致；答案的形态不一致。

  • test@gmial.com（gmail.com 的拼写错误）。两家都抓住了、都建议 gmail.com。Disify 还把 gmial.com 本身标为一次性域名（在它的黑名单里），返回 confidence: 100。EmailCheck 返回裁决 invalid、40 分、纠正后的完整地址 test@gmail.com、域名年龄（8,596 天）和 catch-all 风险 unknown。
  • someone@gmail.com。EmailCheck：100 分、裁决 valid、catch-all 风险 low。Disify：各标记都干净（dns true、disposable false、free true）——但它的 confidence 字段对这个地址是 0，对上下两个坏地址都是 100。
  • user@mailinator.com（一次性域名）。两家都标了一次性。EmailCheck：裁决 risky、50 分、catch-all 风险 possible。Disify：disposable: true 并带黑名单信号。

一句对 EmailCheck 有利、一句不利，都照实说。有利：分数和裁决每档都替你算好。不利：对 mailinator 它顶层仍返回 valid: true、同时给 risky 裁决——要读裁决和分数，别读那个布尔值。

Where Disify is strongerDisify 更强的地方

  • Volume economics. 30,000 free validations a month is thirty times EmailCheck's free tier, and paid plans remove the cap entirely — at $19/month, high-volume verification is simply cheaper there.
  • Zero-friction start. No account, no key: the API answers anonymous calls immediately.
  • Bulk shape. 12,000–100,000 addresses per call is a different class from 100 per call.
  • Deeper domain checks on Pro. SPF/DKIM/DMARC posture, deliverability score, spam-trap detection and privacy-relay detection are real features EmailCheck does not have.
  • 量的账。每月 30,000 次免费是 EmailCheck 免费档的 30 倍，付费档直接不限量——$19/月，大批量验证在它那边就是更便宜。
  • 零摩擦开始。不用注册、不用 Key，匿名调用立刻有答。
  • 批量形态。单次 12,000–100,000 条，和 100 条/次不是一个量级。
  • Pro 档的深度检查。SPF/DKIM/DMARC 配置、投递分、spam trap、隐私中继检测，都是真功能，EmailCheck 没有。

Where EmailCheck is strongerEmailCheck 更强的地方

  • A decision, not just signals. Every response — free tier included — carries a 0–100 score and a valid / risky / invalid verdict your code can branch on immediately.
  • Catch-all risk and domain age in the base price. Disify doesn't list a catch-all signal and puts domain age behind Pro; EmailCheck returns both on every call.
  • Batch results in one response. Up to 100 addresses with full per-address output — no session to babysit, nothing expiring in 15 minutes.
  • A corrected address, not just a domain. The typo flow returns the full corrected address, ready to offer back to the user at signup.

Neither API confirms a mailbox exists — for that you need an SMTP-probing verifier (ZeroBounce, NeverBounce and their peers), at probing prices. The honest stack is: screen at input time with a domain-level API, probe before a big send if the list justifies it.

  • 给结论，不只给信号。每个响应——免费档也一样——带 0–100 分和 valid / risky / invalid 裁决，代码可以直接按裁决分支。
  • catch-all 风险和域名年龄含在基础价里。Disify 没列 catch-all 信号、域名年龄放在 Pro 档；EmailCheck 每次调用都返回这两项。
  • 批量结果一个响应拿齐。一次最多 100 条、每条完整输出——不用守着 session，也没有 15 分钟过期。
  • 返回纠正后的完整地址。拼写流程返回完整纠正地址，注册口可以直接递回给用户确认。

两家都不确认邮箱是否存在——那需要 SMTP 探测型工具（ZeroBounce、NeverBounce 那一类），价格也是探测型的价格。诚实的组合是：输入当下用域名级 API 筛，名单值得的话、大发送前再探测一遍。
